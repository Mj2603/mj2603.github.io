Project 03

Order book engine

Price–time priority matching in C++17, with microstructure signals

LIVE FLOW

Objective

A single-threaded limit order book that is correct first and fast second, for use as the core of market-making and execution simulators.

Design

  • Integer-tick price levels with a FIFO queue each
  • Two-level occupancy bitmap for best bid and ask
  • O(1) cancel through an id → iterator index
  • Bounded SPSC ring for event ingestion

Result

About 10M events/s, with per-event p50 of 89 ns and p99 of 0.50 µs, on a randomized multi-level workload with 20% cancels. That is 85× faster than the earlier linear-scan version.

Verification

Differential test against a std::map reference book (36 seeds, 270k operations), also run under ASan and UBSan. LowLatencyLOBEngine.

    Your resting orders

      The micro-price weights each side of the touch by the size resting on the other side, so it leads the mid when one side is close to depletion. Level-1 imbalance and micro-price skew were among the signal families I tested at Pro-Alpha Capital. A passive order sent from the panel shows its queue position, which improves as orders ahead of it fill or cancel.
