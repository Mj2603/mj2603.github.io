Project 02

Market making

Inventory-aware quoting compared with tuned fixed-spread rules

Objective

Quote two-sided markets that earn the spread without building up directional inventory, and measure what inventory-aware quoting adds over a well-tuned fixed spread.

Method

Avellaneda–Stoikov reservation price and spread, with Poisson fills of intensity A·e−kδ. The repository replays trade prints event by event, calibrates k by maximum likelihood, and walks forward across folds.

Result

Net P&L matches the best fixed rule within one standard error. P&L dispersion is 26–35% lower and peak inventory 22–24% lower over 20 seeds (random-walk and informed flow).

Code

Options-Market-Making-Simulator-. Python, 127 tests. Results are in results/. Run python scripts/run_synthetic_experiment.py to reproduce.
