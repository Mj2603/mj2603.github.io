Project 01

Volatility surface

SABR pricing, calibration and option-book risk

Objective

Price and risk-manage options off a calibrated, arbitrage-checked implied-volatility surface, as on a commodity options desk.

Method

Hagan (2002) lognormal SABR with fixed β and per-expiry α, ρ, ν. Least-squares calibration by bounded Nelder–Mead, and Black-76 pricing on the forward.

Result

Matches an independent Python reference to 1e-11. On a 17-strike smile, calibration recovers the parameters to about 1e-6 in about 0.4 ms. The library also includes binomial, Monte Carlo and Crank–Nicolson engines.

Code

Derivatives-Pricing-Library-C-. C++17, 8 test suites. In practice: SABR and local-vol calibration across 20+ commodity surfaces at Deloitte.

    Negative ρ lifts downside strikes, the usual shape in equity indices and crude oil, while ν controls wing curvature. Vol-of-vol is decayed with expiry, so long-dated smiles flatten at constant ρ. The recalibration panel shows the practical issue: ν is less well identified than ρ, so its day-to-day estimates are noisier for the same quote quality.
