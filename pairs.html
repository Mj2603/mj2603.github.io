Project 04

Statistical arbitrage

Engle–Granger pairs trading, walk-forward without look-ahead

Objective

Find out whether a cointegration pairs strategy keeps an edge once every estimate is restricted to past data and realistic costs are charged.

Method

Engle–Granger and Johansen screening on 252-day training windows, an OLS hedge ratio, a 20-day trailing z-score, entry at |z| ≥ 2 and exit on a zero cross, rolled forward quarterly.

Result

On simulated cointegrated pairs: Sharpe 1.06 before costs, 0.40 at 5 bps and −0.29 at 10 bps. Random-walk controls rarely pass the test and earn nothing. NSE banking-pair results are pending.

Code

Statistical-Arbitrage-Research-. Python, 46 tests, including look-ahead regression tests that perturb future prices and confirm past trades are unchanged.
